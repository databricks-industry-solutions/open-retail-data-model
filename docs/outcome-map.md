# Retail Outcome Map

ORDM's outcome packages follow the retail business outcome map. The 12 first-wave pillars, grouped by macro theme:

## Respond & Predict to Customer Behavior in Real Time
- **Unified Customer View** — Customer 360, Identity Resolution, CDP, Customer Lifetime Value
- **Actionable Customer Understanding** — Behavioral Segmentation, Product Affinity, Market Analysis
- **Agentic Commerce** — Shopping Assistant, Intent Product Discovery, Conversational Shopping, Agent-to-Agent Transactions
- **Commerce Media Networks** — Sponsored Products Placement, In-Store Digital Ads, Incrementality Testing, Cross-Platform Retail Mgmt

## Profitable Volume Growth
- **Balancing Margin and Volume** — Dynamic Price Optimization, Assortment Optimization, Elasticity Modeling
- **Promote with Purpose** — Trade Promotion, Location-Based Offers, Post-Promotion ROI Analysis

## Collaborative Supply Chain
- **Early Risk Detection** — Real-Time Disruption Detection, Supplier Score & Monitoring, Procurement Risk Detection
- **Smarter Demand and Inventory Decisions** — Demand & Order Forecasting, Inventory Optimization, Out-of-Stocks Optimization
- **Data Sharing with Suppliers** — Media Measurement, Data Monetization, Joint Demand Planning, Category Growth

## Intelligent In-Store Experience
- **Connected Store Signals** — Smart Shelf, RFID Tracking, Foot Traffic Analysis, IoT Sensor Monitoring
- **Predictive Operations** — Store Performance, Workforce Optimization, In-Store Equipment Maintenance
- **AI-Assisted Store Associates** — Associates Best Next Actions, Real-Time Data Access, Digital Assistant Selling Tools
