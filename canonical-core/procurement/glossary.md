# Procurement Domain — Business Glossary

> Status: ✅ Published (0.5)

| Term | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Purchase order line** (`purchase_order_line`) | One line of a purchase order placed with a supplier (order + receipt + defect + price), at PO × line grain. Amounts are in the reporting/base currency. Links to `supplier` and `product`. |
